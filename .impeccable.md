# Design Context — Bo7

**Target audience**: Adults 30–60 in south Dublin (Blackrock, Killiney, Dalkey, Glenageary, Dún Laoghaire). Many are nervous beginners or people returning to exercise after a break; some are regular trainers who want coaching. They browse on their phone, often from an Instagram or Google Maps link.

**Use cases**: Decide whether Bo7 is "for me", pick the nearer location, book a free consultation (Glofox lead form), or call/WhatsApp. Secondary: check timetable, download the app.

**Brand personality/tone**: Confident, warm, straight-talking coach. Premium coaching without intimidation. Community and fun are real differentiators (beach sessions, hikes, parties). Line: "Future Proof Your Body". Not a bro gym, not a boutique class studio.

**Visual identity to keep**: Black + gold (#BB9807), Archivo Black display type, the pro photo shoot (`source/images/_S7V*.jpg`).

**Constraints**: Static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, no build step. Lead capture stays in Glofox (two branches). Must be fast on mobile. Hosted at bo7.thedowlings.co first, then bo7.ie.
