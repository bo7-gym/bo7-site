## What we collect and why

**When you ask about joining.** If you fill in the "Book a free consultation" form, your name, phone number, email and anything you tell us go to Glofox, the booking system we use, so we can contact you and arrange your consultation. If you message us on WhatsApp or call, we see your number and what you send. Legal basis: steps you ask us to take before becoming a member. **[CHECK]**

**Website analytics.** With your permission, we use Google Analytics to see how people find bo7.ie and which pages and buttons they use (for example, whether someone tapped WhatsApp or the timetable). This uses cookies. If you choose "Reject", or don't choose, Google Analytics runs without cookies and only sends basic, anonymous counts. You can change your choice any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. Legal basis: your consent.

**Maps and videos.** The location maps are embedded from Google Maps, and member videos play from YouTube when you tap them. Google may set its own cookies when these load. **[CHECK: whether to put maps behind a click too]**

**Members area.** If you're a Bo7 member, the members area at bo7.ie/members stores your name, mobile number, home gym, attendance (from Glofox), and the personal bests, milestones and workout notes you choose to log. Only you can see your own entries, plus Bo7 coaches at your gym for anything you choose to share with them. We text you a code to log in for the first time. Legal basis: providing the membership service you signed up for. **[CHECK]**

## Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We use these services to run Bo7, and they handle data on our behalf:

- **Glofox (ABC Fitness):** bookings, membership and leads. **[CHECK where Glofox stores data]**
- **Supabase:** the members area database, hosted in the EU (Ireland).
- **Twilio:** sends login codes by text.
- **Google:** Analytics, Maps, YouTube, and Bo7 email (Google Workspace).
- **Cloudflare:** a bot check on the members login page.
- **GitHub:** hosts the bo7.ie website.

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how we use it. Members can download their members-area data any time from **Me → Download my data**.
