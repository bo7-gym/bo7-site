Step 1 · Position

Ask the members why they chose Bo7

13 questions, about 4 minutes

  • Who they are: gym, how long, age and gender
  • What they did before, and what made them look for something new
  • Who else they considered, and why Bo7 won
  • What nearly stopped them joining
  • What has changed for them since
  • Bo7 in one sentence, in their own words

How it runs

  1. Claude builds the Google Form in about 10 minutes
  2. Ryan posts it in the members' WhatsApp group
  3. Open for seven days, with a reminder on day three
  4. Claude reads the answers alongside the Google reviews

Their words become the copy. Members who opt in to be quoted or filmed become the proof on the next slide.
