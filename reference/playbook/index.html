How to use this playbook

Two rules, and one setup step

Each step has what to do, who does it, and the prompts to paste into Claude.

  1. Every prompt starts from the Bo7 brand position. Paste it in, or keep it in a Claude Project, so every output sounds like Bo7 and not a generic gym.
  2. A person approves anything before it is sent. Claude drafts; Ryan and the coaches decide and send.

Setup: the prompts are written for Claude Code in the Claude desktop app, with the Claude in Chrome extension connected. That lets Claude work in your own browser: clicking through settings pages like DNS, Google profiles, Google Forms and ad accounts while you watch.

  • Connect Google Drive to Claude with the Bo7 Google account (Settings, Connectors). Claude can then read the survey results, the nightly copy of the Bo7 database and other Bo7 files directly.
  • You sign in to every site yourself. Claude never types a password.
  • Claude shows you what it is about to change and waits for your OK before it saves, sends or deletes anything.
  • Keep everything in one Bo7 folder that Claude Code works in (the bo7-site project is a good home): the brand position, the scorecard and any exports. Then a prompt can say "using the project files" instead of pasting everything in.

The consultation is already Bo7's strength. This step makes sure every lead reaches it and nothing is lost after it: a board the team works every morning, people turning up, outcomes recorded, the not-yets followed up, and new members welcomed.

1 Build the leads board, and work it every morningEvery lead in play and a one-tap reply, with prompt 3.1

One page that shows every lead by stage, where they came from, how long they have waited, and buttons to WhatsApp, call or email them. Open a lead to book their consult and keep notes. Working through it every morning is how nobody slips through.

The design is already mocked up with sample data at bo7.thedowlings.co/dash; click a lead there to see the booking dialog. Prompt 3.1 builds the real version to match.

  1. Run prompt 3.1. Claude builds the board at team.bo7.ie/leads, on top of the Bo7 database. It sits behind the Cloudflare login, so only @bo7.ie email addresses get in, because it shows personal details.
  2. Each coach opens team.bo7.ie/leads once, logs in with their @bo7.ie email, and adds it to their phone's home screen.
  3. Every morning: start with anyone waiting over 24 hours, then follow-ups due today, then upcoming consults. The WhatsApp button opens the chat with the right script already typed.
  4. To book a consult, open the lead: pick the gym, a coach (or any coach) and one of the free times. It goes straight into the Bo7 consultations calendar and the database, and adds a note. Then tap the WhatsApp button to send the confirmation, already written.
  5. Add a note whenever you speak to someone: goals, injuries, best times to reach them. Every note is kept, with who wrote it and when.
  6. Update the lead as you go (Contacted, Showed up, Joined, Not yet). It writes straight to the database, so the numbers stay right without extra typing.
  7. If someone never gets back to you, or the number is wrong, open the lead and choose Remove from board: Couldn't reach them, Not interested right now, or Don't contact again. They stay in the database with that status and a note, and come back onto the board in 1, 3 or 6 months to try again, if they agreed to hear from Bo7.
