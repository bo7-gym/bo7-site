Set up the Bo7 database: one place where every lead, note, consult, member and win-back message lives, so the leads board, the consult page, the retain page, the scheduled jobs and the reports all work from the same records. Bo7 is a semi-private personal training gym with two locations in south Dublin, Killiney and Blackrock.

Use Cloudflare D1, inside the bo7-team project. If Cloudflare lets you keep the database in the EU, do that, and tell me what you chose. Leads and members are personal data.

Rules: never put a password, key or token in the code. Never invent leads, members, coaches or numbers. Show me the schema before you create anything.

1. Create a D1 database called bo7, bound to the bo7-team Pages project. Keep every schema change as a numbered migration file in the project, so the history is clear and the database can be rebuilt.

2. Tables. Use clear names, created and updated timestamps on every row, and IDs that never change.
   - sources: code, label, description. Fill with: walk-in, phone, whatsapp, email, website-form, killiney-window, blackrock-window, stand, referral, corporate, instagram, meta-ads, google-ads, google-profile-killiney, google-profile-blackrock, other.
   - coaches: name, email (@bo7.ie), gyms, active.
   - consult_hours: coach, gym, day of week, from, to. Plus a setting for consult length in minutes.
   - leads: name, phone, email, preferred contact (whatsapp, call, email), gym, source, source detail, interested in, logged by, consent to contact (yes or no, and when it was given), status (new, contacted, consult_booked, joined, not_now, couldnt_reach, lost), first reply at, follow up on, reason not joined (price, timing, location, not_ready, chose_elsewhere, wanted_one_to_one, other).
   - notes: lead or member, written by, text. Notes are only ever added, never edited.
   - consults: lead, gym, coach, starts at, calendar event ID, status (booked, moved, cancelled, done), showed up, outcome (joined, thinking, not_yet, no_show), and the structured consult notes: goals, training so far, injuries or health, days that suit, times that suit, anything else.
   - members: Glofox member ID, name, phone, email, gym, coach, membership, joined on, left on, consent to contact, linked lead (if they came through the leads board).
   - visits: member, date and time, booked or attended or no-show, from Glofox.
   - member_health: one row per member per day: days since last visit, visits in the last 4 weeks, visits in the 4 weeks before, health (healthy, slowing, at_risk). Keep the history; the dashboard's "4 weeks ago" figures come from it.
   - winback: member, sequence (slowing or at_risk), step, started at, next step at, status (active, paused, completed, exited_trained, removed, lapsed), paused until.
   - messages: who it went to, channel, template, sent by (a coach or automatic), sent at, result. Every outbound message is logged here.
   - leavers: member, left on, reason, would come back, notes.
   - settings: health thresholds (days for slowing, days for at risk, the drop that counts as slowing), sequence timings, how long to keep leads who never joined.
   - audit_log: who changed what and when, for every write.
