Using the Bo7 brand position and the member survey analysis in the project files, help Bo7 publish one member story a month: a short video and a written version, for Instagram, the website and the ads.

1. Who: suggest candidates from members who opted in to telling their story on video (survey question 16) and whose consult notes show a clear "before" (their goals) and a clear "now". Mix gyms, ages and starting points over the year. List names for Ryan to approve; don't contact anyone.
2. The interview: 8 short questions that bring out the story in their own words (where they were, what made them start, what nearly stopped them, what changed, what they'd tell someone like them). Under 10 minutes.
3. The shoot: a shot list for a phone in portrait: one at the gym training, one talking to camera, a few close-ups. Under 30 minutes, natural light, no music needed.
4. Consent: a short plain-English consent form for using their name, video and words on Instagram, the website and ads, with how to withdraw it. Store the signed copy in the Bo7 Drive.
5. The outputs from each interview transcript: a 45 to 60 second video script cut from their own words, an Instagram caption, a 120-word written story for the website's results section, and one quote for ads.

Use members' own words. Never exaggerate results or add claims they didn't make.
