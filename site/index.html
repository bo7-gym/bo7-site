Questions people ask before they join

Honest answers.

What if I'm nervous about joining?

That's completely normal, and it's most people's starting point. You don't need to be fit, confident or experienced. Your coach explains every exercise, sets the weight for you, and progresses you at your own pace. Nobody is watching you except the person paid to help.

Is it suitable for beginners?

Yes. We coach people who have never lifted a weight, people coming back after years away, and people who train regularly. Your sessions are adapted to where you are now.

What is semi-private personal training?

It's personal training in a small group. You follow a programme designed for your goals, and a coach guides you through every session, with the motivation of a few other people training beside you.

How is it different from a normal gym?

In a normal gym you get access and a membership card. At Bo7 you get a structured programme, a coach at every session, and a small group that notices when you don't show up. We keep session sizes small so you get real attention.

I have a specific goal. Will it work for that?

Yes. Strength, fitness, weight loss, mobility, getting back into a routine, or simply becoming consistent. Your coach adapts your training to the goal, and we check in on how it's going.

How often should I train?

It depends on your goals, your fitness and your week. We help you find a frequency that's realistic and sustainable, then build a plan around it. Consistency beats intensity.
