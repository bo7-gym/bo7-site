## Semi-Private Personal Training in Blackrock & Killiney
### FUTURE PROOF YOUR BODY
Bo7 is a semi-private personal training gym with locations in Blackrock and Killiney, Dublin. We combine the personal attention of one-to-one coaching with the motivation, energy and community of small group training.
Forget generic gym programmes and trying to figure out what to do next. Our experienced coaches create structured training designed around your goals, your current ability and the life you want to live.
